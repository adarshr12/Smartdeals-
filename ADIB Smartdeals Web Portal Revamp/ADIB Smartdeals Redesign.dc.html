6v3 · Light, minimal, guest-first, built to work at any zoom level

The web portal has no login, so every visitor starts as a first-time guest and sees the standard lists. Signing in is optional and adds the personalised lists. There are no habit features and no visit history. The page is light, with only the navy header. The banner keeps its fixed size, with search and the main navigation right next to it. Every layout adjusts to the browser width. Below you’ll see it at 100%, at 130% zoom (~1108px) and at 80% zoom (~1800px).

6aGuest homepage · 1440 (100%)
6bSigned in (optional) · personalised lists appear
6cBrowser at 130% zoom · 1108px
  • Lists fill the width: each list shows one row and fits as many 232px+ cards as the width allows: 5 at 100%, 4 at 130%. At 80% the content stays centred at 1360px with wider side margins, so there are still 5 cards and line lengths stay readable. Cards never squeeze.
  • Spacing scales with the screen (between 16 and 40px at the sides, 36 to 56px between sections), so it never feels cramped or sparse.
  • Banner: a fixed 1280×320 slot that shrinks to fit and never crops. Its controls sit below it, so nothing covers the creative. The size is a tweak if yours differs.
  • One focus per screen: search, then the banner, then categories, then lists. Each list has one coloured icon and an optional line under the title. Reason strings only appear when signed in.
  • Offers near you: a light map next to the 4 nearest offers, with Open full map and Change area. It sits after the first list, with plenty of space around it.
  • Maya: appears as the “Ask Maya” option inside search and as a small floating avatar button.

4v2 · Modern homepage, search, All offers with sort, map view

Personalised lists can’t be sorted, so sort lives only on the new All offers page. That page has no lists, just filters, sort, and a grid or map view. The homepage moves to a darker look with a soft moving glow and a bento layout. Each list type has its own colour. Expiring offers are red, and the red dot pulses.

4aHomepage v2 · full scroll
4bSearch open · typed “Piz”
4cAll offers · grid, Sort open
4dAll offers · map view (live map, try panning)
  • List colours: For you = blue · Near you = teal · Your taste = violet · Ending soon = red · Today only = pink · Trending = navy · Time-based = orange. Each list has an icon tile, a label and a light tint behind it.
  • Urgency: red for offers ending within 7 days, with a pulsing dot; solid red for “Ends today”. Dates are real, never a fake timer.
  • Recall: “Pick up where you left off” shows recently viewed offers, codes you copied, and past searches. It’s stored on this device and has a Clear history link.
  • Habits: Thursday Drop with a live countdown to the next real drop and a Remind me button, a weekday lunch pick, and a nudge before saved offers end. Each has its own switch, and you can see which days you visited this week. Nothing is chance-based.
  • Search: suggestions as you type, with matches in bold, merchant value and urgency, plus categories, an “Ask Maya” shortcut, recent and trending searches, and keyboard hints.
  • Map: a live map of Abu Dhabi with price pins and your position. The pins drop in, the list and map stay in sync, and there’s a Search this area button. A small dark version of the map is on the homepage.
  • Sort: only on All offers. “See all” on a list opens that list in order, without sort.
  • Motion: cards rise in one after another, the background glow drifts slowly, Maya floats gently, search and popovers slide down, the urgency dot pulses. All of it switches off under reduced motion.
  • Filters: a sticky filter bar with icons, a side panel with counts and a distance slider, and removable filter pills next to the result count.
2New · Maya assistant, temporal rail, social proof, like / not interested

There is no login, so the savings snapshot is gone. Its place goes to Just ask Maya. Maya is more than a chat window: she moves to the element you need and highlights it. Personal savings are replaced by redemption proxies with no numbers attached: “People near you are redeeming this”, “Popular with people like you”. The first rail is the temporal contextual list.
